## 1. Introduction

### Objective of the Portfolio
The primary objective of this portfolio is to showcase my skills, educational background, and completed projects in a professional, interactive, and visually appealing manner. It serves as a digital resume that allows potential employers and collaborators to easily access my work and contact me.

### Brief Introduction to HCI and Why it Matters in Web Design
Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) is the study of how people interact with computers and to what extent computers are or are not developed for successful interaction with human beings. In web design, applying HCI principles is crucial because it ensures the website is intuitive, accessible, and user-friendly. A good HCI approach reduces the cognitive load on the user, making navigation seamless and improving the overall user experience (UX).

## 3. HCI Techniques and Rules Applied

### User-Centered Design Process
The design process was highly focused on the end-user. Information was structured logically, prioritizing the "Home" and "About" sections so users can quickly understand who I am. The navigation is always visible, allowing the user to move freely without getting lost.

### HCI Rules/Heuristics Applied (Nielsen's Heuristics)
1. **Consistency and Standards:** 
   *Example:* The navigation bar is placed consistently at the top of every page. The color scheme (dark background with blue `#4da6ff` accents) and typography are consistent across all sections, reducing user confusion.
2. **Aesthetic and Minimalist Design:** 
   *Example:* The user interface does not contain information that is irrelevant. The "Projects" and "Education" pages use clean cards with proper spacing (margins and paddings) to present information without overwhelming the user.
3. **User Control and Freedom:** 
   *Example:* The use of React Router provides an instant, smooth transition between pages. Users can freely switch between 'Home', 'Projects', and 'Contact' at any time without using the browser's back button.

### Accessibility and Responsiveness Features
- **Responsiveness:** React Bootstrap grid (`<Container>`, `<Row>`, `<Col>`) was used to ensure that the layout adapts perfectly to mobile, tablet, and desktop screens. For example, project cards stack vertically on small screens and sit side-by-side on larger screens.
- **Accessibility (Contrast):** A high-contrast dark theme was chosen (black background with white/light gray text and bright blue accents). This reduces eye strain and makes the text highly readable for visually impaired users.

## 5. Evaluation & Reflection

### Ensuring a Good User Experience
A good user experience was ensured by prioritizing readability and fast navigation. By replacing heavy SVG codes with optimized `react-icons`, page loading and code maintainability were improved. The contact form was properly spaced out to avoid accidental clicks, and social links were made clear and recognizable.

### Challenges Faced & How They Were Overcome
One challenge was managing the layout of project cards and the contact form to look good on both large screens and mobile phones. This was overcome by utilizing Bootstrap's responsive grid system (`lg={4}`, `md={6}`) and adjusting CSS flexbox properties (`justify-content-center`) to ensure items aligned perfectly regardless of the screen size.

### Improvements I Would Like to Make
In the future, I would like to add a functional backend to the Contact form so that messages are sent directly to my email. I would also like to implement subtle micro-animations (like page transition fades) to make the browsing experience feel even more dynamic and premium.
